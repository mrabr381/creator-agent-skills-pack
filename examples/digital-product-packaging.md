# Worked Example — digital-product-packaging

> **Sample data.** The product below is fictional, written for this
> example. Prices and contents are illustrative, not a recommendation.

## Input

- **Buyer questions (real, collected from comments):** "How do I plan a
  month of videos without burning out?" (asked 6 times), "What do I post
  when I have no ideas?" (4 times), "How do I keep a consistent schedule?"
  (3 times).
- **Buyer:** New YouTubers posting 2 videos a week.
- **Promise:** "After this planner, you can plan a month of videos in one
  afternoon."

## Outline (one chapter per repeated question)

1. The month-at-a-glance board — plan 8 videos in one sitting.
2. The idea bank — 40 prompts for days with no ideas.
3. The steady schedule — a 2-videos-a-week routine that survives busy weeks.

## Listing (excerpt)

**The Small-Channel Planner — plan a month of videos in one afternoon,
for new YouTubers posting twice a week.**

- **Price:** $19 — includes the planner PDF, editable source, quick-start
  sheet, and 40 idea prompts.

## Checks passed

Chapters map to the collected questions; no filler chapter; previews are
real pages; price, contents and refund policy stated plainly; no fake
scarcity and no "was $99" discount.
