# Worked Example — youtube-outlier-research

> **Sample data.** The channel below is fictional ("Harbour Histories").
> All numbers are invented for this example only — they demonstrate the
> maths, and must never be quoted as real measurements.

## Input

- **Niche:** British coastal history mysteries, 5–12 minute faceless videos.
- **Channel:** Harbour Histories (fictional) — 12,400 subscribers.
- **Collection date:** 2026-11-20. Channel median views (last 20 videos): **9,000**.

Maths shown: 95,000 ÷ 9,000 = 10.6; 48,000 ÷ 9,000 = 5.3;
22,000 ÷ 9,000 = 2.4. Velocity: 95,000 ÷ 30 days = 3,167/day.

## Patterns in the top 3 (outlier score ≥ 2.0x)

- Named person, place, or specific thing in title: 3/3
- Mystery / secret / disappearance: 3/3
- Under 12 minutes: 3/3
- Pure place tour (no mystery): 0/3 — the tour video (#4) scored only 1.2x

## Video ideas (with evidence)

1. **The Ferryman Who Never Came Back** — named person + disappearance,
   following videos #1 and #2.
2. **The Cellar Beneath the Customs House** — hidden place + secret,
   following video #2.
3. **Three Wrecks the Tide Gives Back** — numbered list + named wrecks,
   following video #3.
