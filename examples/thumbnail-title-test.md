# Worked Example — thumbnail-title-test

> **Sample data.** The video below is fictional. Scores are illustrative
> judgements for this example, not measured results.

## Input

- **Video:** 8-minute video about a lighthouse keeper who vanished in 1912.
- **Payoff (first 30 seconds):** the lighthouse was found empty, but the
  lamp was still burning — and the logbook's last entry is read aloud.
- **Named subject:** the lighthouse keeper of 1912.

## Title variants, scored (rubric: curiosity / named subject / payoff honesty / readability, each 0–5)

| # | Title | Curiosity | Named | Honesty | Readability | Total |
|---|-------|-----------|-------|---------|-------------|-------|
| A | The Lighthouse Keeper Who Vanished — But the Lamp Was Still Burning | 5 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 18/20 |
| B | Why Did Nobody Find Him for 40 Years? | 4 | 5 | 5 | 3 | 17/20 |
| C | Lighthouse History Explained | 3 | 2 | 5 | 5 | 15/20 |

Maths shown: A = 5+5+4+4 = 18; B = 4+5+5+3 = 17; C = 3+2+5+5 = 15.

## Chosen pair

- **Title A (18/20)** — highest score; the payoff (burning lamp) is in
  the video's first 30 seconds, so the promise is honest.
- **Thumbnail concept:** the lighthouse at night, lamp glowing, keeper's
  empty chair in the foreground; text: "STILL BURNING" (2 words).
